QR ATTENDANCE - DFPLAYER MINI AUDIO

Copy the entire mp3 folder to the ROOT of your DFPlayer Mini microSD card.
The result should be: SD card / mp3 / 0001.mp3 (and the other tracks).
Use a supported FAT16/FAT32 card, up to 32GB. If formatting is necessary,
back up your existing card files first: formatting erases the card.
Power off the DFPlayer before inserting or removing its card.

FILES USED BY THE CURRENT FIRMWARE
0001.mp3 - Your attendance has been recorded. (Present, Late and Absent)
0002.mp3 - Your attendance is already recorded.
0003.mp3 - Invalid QR card. Please ask your teacher.
0004.mp3 - Attendance was not saved. Please try again.

OPTIONAL STATUS ANNOUNCEMENTS
0005.mp3 - Present. Your attendance has been recorded.
0006.mp3 - Late. Your attendance has been recorded.
0007.mp3 - Absent. Please see your teacher.
Tracks 0005-0007 require a firmware update to select them by attendance status.
They will not play automatically with the current firmware.

All files are real MP3 audio: 44.1kHz, mono, 128kbps.
English speech generated locally using Microsoft Zira Desktop.
Keep the four-digit names and avoid duplicate extensions like 0001.mp3.mp3.
The card goes into the DFPlayer Mini, not the ESP32-CAM card slot.
