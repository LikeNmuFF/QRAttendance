DFPLAYER MINI - MALE VOICE - NO FIRMWARE UPDATE REQUIRED

Copy the mp3 folder directly to the ROOT of your 8 GB FAT32 microSD card.
Example: SD card / mp3 / 0001.mp3
Replace the same four tracks already on the card. Power off before card removal/insertion.
Insert the card into the DFPlayer Mini, then power the scanner on again.

0001.mp3: Your attendance has been recorded.
0002.mp3: Your attendance has already been recorded.
0003.mp3: Invalid QR card. Please use your official attendance QR card.
0004.mp3: Your attendance could not be saved. Please try again or contact your instructor.

The current firmware uses 0001 for Present, Late AND Absent.
Separate announcements for those statuses require a firmware change and reflash.
Track 0004 is shared by several failure conditions.
There is no automatic startup playback in the current firmware.
If camera initialization fails, the firmware halts before scanning and no track is played.

Voice: Microsoft en-US-GuyNeural, synthetic male English voice, rate -5%.
Audio: real MPEG MP3, 44.1 kHz, mono, 128 kbps.
All four files passed an FFmpeg decode check. Physical speaker playback was not tested.
